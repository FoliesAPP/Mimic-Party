### *Hear it. Mimic it. Pray nobody laughs.*

**Mimic Party** is a chaotic **1–5 player online voice party game** where everyone hears the same sound and gets **one single shot** to reproduce it with their voice.

No turns.  
No rehearsals.  
No retries.  
No mercy.

Your performance is judged on **pitch, rhythm, and attacks** — then a completely unreasonable wheel decides who gets rewarded and whose masterpiece gets turned into a fart.

</div>

## 🎤 The Party Starts With One Sound

Everyone hears a reference sound.

Then the countdown begins.

Everyone performs **at the same time**.

Your microphone captures your attempt, the local AI analyzes how closely you reproduced the sound, and then the entire table gets to hear the results.

There is no second attempt.

There is no undo.

Whatever came out of your mouth...

**that's the take.**

# ✦ WE DON'T CARE ABOUT YOUR VOICE ✦

The game isn't interested in whether you naturally have a deep voice, a high voice, or the vocal range of a dying kazoo.

**Mimic Party scores the shape of your performance.**

It focuses on elements that humans can actually reproduce:

- 🎵 Melody
- 🥁 Rhythm
- ⚡ Attacks
- ⏱️ Timing

A high voice and a low voice can perform the same sound and receive comparable scores.

The question isn't:

> **"Do you sound like the original?"**

It's:

> **"Did you actually mimic it?"**

---

# ✦ THE WHEEL DECIDES ✦

Between rounds, the wheel spins.

And the wheel has absolutely no respect for your friendships.

It can award:

### 🟢 Bonuses
Extra points and rewards.

### 🟡 Multipliers
Because apparently you weren't already competitive enough.

### 🔴 Sabotages
Choose another player and ruin their next performance.

Possible effects include:

- Saturation
- Echo
- Pitch shifting
- Chopped audio
- Audio distortion
- Replacing their take with a completely different sound

And sometimes...

### 💨 THE FART

Your beautiful vocal performance can simply be replaced by a fart.

The table hears the fart.

You lose points.

Everyone laughs.

**This is Mimic Party.**

---

# ✦ THE ROOM GOES QUIET ✦

Voice chat is active throughout the game.

Except when someone performs.

The instant a recorded take starts playing:

**everyone's microphones go quiet.**

The entire table hears the exact same performance.

Then the recording ends.

The microphones open.

And the room explodes.

That tiny moment between **hearing the result** and **everyone reacting** is where the game lives.

# ✦ BRING YOUR OWN NOISES ✦

The sounds don't have to stop with the default content.

**Mimic Party supports Steam Workshop sound packs**, allowing the community to create and share their own collections.

Create a sound pack.

Publish it.

Subscribe to someone else's.

Then force your friends to imitate whatever you've decided is funny.

Everyone in the lobby needs the required pack before the game starts, with the lobby showing who is still downloading it.

---

# ✦ A PERFECT PARTY GAME ✦

Mimic Party is designed around short, chaotic sessions.

### **~10 MINUTES**

Long enough to:

- embarrass yourself
- destroy a friendship
- discover someone is inexplicably good at mimicking engines
- get sabotaged
- retaliate
- laugh uncontrollably
- play another round

Short enough that **"one more game"** is always a reasonable lie.
